# Letras ilustradas KARTHA

Atlas: letras_kartha.png. Seis recortes normalizados: letra_0.png até letra_5.png. Recurso: spr_titulo_kartha_letras, frames K A R T H A.

Criado com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, usando a imagem fornecida pelo usuário como referência. É uma recriação ilustrada, não a identificação de uma fonte tipográfica. O atlas foi separado e normalizado para importação. Apenas as seis letras do título usam estes sprites; botões, cartas e movimentos existentes foram preservados.

Prompt: Create a transparent production sprite atlas of exactly SIX SEPARATE uppercase hand-drawn fantasy letters, in this exact order: K A R T H A. Use the attached KARTHA logo solely as a style reference: jagged thorn-like organic silhouette, sharp crooked terminals, strange medieval horror lettering, a small eye integrated into the T and subtle web details in R/H. Reinterpret the logo into standalone individual glyphs, NOT connected logo art. Bright warm ivory filled lettering with thin charcoal outlines, readable on dark brown cards. Flat ink illustration, no metallic realism, no pixel art, no shadows, no gradients, no backgrounds, no frames, no buttons. CRITICAL ATLAS LAYOUT: wide landscape canvas aspect ratio 3:1, six equal-width vertical cells in ONE horizontal row. Each glyph is completely contained in its own cell, centered at 1/12,3/12,5/12,7/12,9/12,11/12 of canvas width. All have consistent visual height ~70% of canvas height, except T may extend slightly lower. Keep at least 12% of each cell's width as blank transparent margin on either side. No cell borders or guides. The two A letters should match. Only six glyphs, no other marks. This will be sliced into six equal width images for six floating cards.
